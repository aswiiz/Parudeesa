Our Heritage

A Serene Lakeside Sanctuary

Nestled on the serene banks of the lake, Parudeesa Lakeside Restaurant offers an idyllic escape inspired by classical Kerala models. Our architectural theme pays homage to the legendary heritage of the region, featuring an authentic "ola menja melkoora" (thatched roof) aesthetic that harmonizes beautifully with the lush tropical surroundings.

Whether you are hosting grand celebrations, intimate family functions, or wishing to enjoy our curated culinary delights from the comfort of your home with our dedicated food delivery service, Parudeesa provides an unparalleled experience that nourishes both body and mind.
